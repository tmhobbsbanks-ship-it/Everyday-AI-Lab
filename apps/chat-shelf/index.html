Bring your chats in

Each app emails you a download link. Save the file to Files on your iPad, then choose it below. Your chats stay on this iPad.

ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Data controls → Export data
  2. Open the email, download the .zip
  3. Choose the .zip here

Claude

  1. Settings → Privacy → Export data
  2. Download the .zip from the email
  3. Choose the .zip here

Gemini

  1. Go to takeout.google.com
  2. Deselect all, then tick My Activity and pick only Gemini Apps
  3. Choose the .zip here

Perplexity

Perplexity has no full-history export. Open a thread, tap Share → Export → Markdown, then choose the files here (several at once is fine). Or paste a thread below.
